He was the greatest fool in the kingdom... sitting on a branch, and cutting it down.
Cunning ministers tricked him into marrying Princess Vidyottama, the wisest scholar in the land.
The moment his ignorance was revealed, she cast him out of the palace in shame.
Broken, he wandered into an abandoned temple... the shrine of Maa Kali.
He fell at her feet and whispered, "Maa... I have nothing. Only my devotion."
All night he chanted her name, never moving, never stopping.
The wind went still... and the diyas burst into flame.
A blinding light filled the temple... and Maa Kali appeared.
One glance from the goddess, and the darkness of his mind was gone.
She touched his tongue, and blessed him with knowledge and the power of words.
That fool rose as Kalidasa... the servant of Kali, and the greatest poet in Sanskrit history.
When devotion is true... the Mother never sends her child away empty-handed.
